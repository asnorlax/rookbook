The mates you never saw.

A forced mate was on the board and you played something else. It happens far more than anyone thinks — and you never find out, because the game moves on and nobody tells you. rookbook scans every game in your history, finds the ones where a mate was available, and replays each to the exact position with the move you missed and the move you played instead.

Including the games you won. Those are the instructive ones: a mate in two you walked past on move 20 is the same blind spot that will cost you a winning position next week, whether or not you got away with it this time.

What you hand over: a username.

Every other tool wants an account first. This one is a web page that reads public data and does the work on your own machine.

01

Type your username

That's the whole sign-up. No account, no password, no email address, nothing to cancel later.

02

It reads your public history

The same games anyone can already see on your Chess.com profile, pulled straight from Chess.com's public API. Everything is worked out from the moves themselves, so it covers every game you've played — not only the ones you've run Game Review on. Accuracy is the single exception, since Chess.com publishes that only for reviewed games.

03

The work happens on your device

Analysis runs in your browser and the results are cached there. Your games are never uploaded to us, because there's no us to upload them to.
