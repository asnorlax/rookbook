Everything Rookbook tracks

Rookbook reads your Chess.com history and builds a profile you can't get anywhere else. Every feature below runs in your browser — even the engine — with no account, and no data leaves your machine.

Chess.com gives you one Game Review a day, then asks for a membership. Blown Leads

Games where you held a winning material advantage and lost anyway. Every game is tracked with your peak lead, how much you gave back, and engine evaluation of where it went wrong. This is the gap between being ahead and staying ahead — and it shows you exactly what it costs.

Missed Checkmates

Every position where a forced mate in one was on the board and you played something else. The scan covers your entire history, not just games you ran through Game Review. It separates the misses that cost you the game from the ones that only delayed the win, so you know which ones actually matter.
